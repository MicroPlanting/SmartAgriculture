    Emergency protection

    An emergency exists when temperature > 35 °C or raw gas reading > 1000. Comparisons are strict: 35 °C and a gas reading of 1000 are not emergencies; 36 °C and 1001 are.

    Missing or invalid readings never count as an emergency. Dry soil never triggers the buzzer.

    The ESP32 must use the same limits to sound the physical buzzer. This website only displays and, optionally, beeps in your browser; it cannot confirm the physical buzzer.

    ENGINEERED FOR A GREENER TOMORROW

    Watering by hand is easy to get wrong: too much wastes water, too little stresses the plant. MicroPlanting measures soil moisture with an analog sensor, and an ESP32 switches a pump through an L298N driver when the soil reads dry.

    The ESP32 also reads temperature and humidity (DHT11) and a gas sensor, opens a servo window when the air is very humid, and sounds a buzzer if temperature or gas passes its limit. Readings go to Firebase Realtime Database and appear here after sign-in.

    This is a student project. It makes no claims about water savings or crop yield; its goal is to show how electronics and software work together.
